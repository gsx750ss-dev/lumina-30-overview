# LUMINA-30 Consultation and Routing Map

Status: non-binding public reference. This page is not a help desk, legal advice, certification process, official adoption channel, enforcement mechanism, deployment approval, or safety guarantee.

Use this page when a reader, reviewer, auditor, policy team, procurement team, AI assistant, or affected stakeholder is unsure where a LUMINA-30-related question should go.

## Core routing question

> What kind of decision is being made, and which review path can preserve effective human refusal before irreversible consequences occur?

LUMINA-30 does not replace technical safety review, legal review, security review, ethics review, incident investigation, or public accountability. It routes the boundary question to the correct review layer.

## Fast routing table

| If the question is... | Route it to... | Use LUMINA-30 to ask... |
|---|---|---|
| Is this release, deployment, exposure, connection, publication, or handoff safe to proceed? | Pre-Incident Boundary Review and responsible release review | Can effective human refusal still be exercised before this opens an irreversible path? |
| What evidence proves that refusal was real rather than symbolic? | Evidence Requirements | Who could understand, refuse, and actually stop the action before irreversibility? |
| Which compact working set should a reviewer use? | Practical Boundary Review Pack | What is the boundary action, evidence block, decision tag, and AI-assistance limit? |
| How can this connect to audits, procurement, contracts, insurance, or regulation? | Institutional Adoption Path | What level of institutional use is being claimed, and what evidence supports it? |
| Did an AI-related incident, near miss, oversight failure, or post-incident evidence gap occur? | Incident Review workflow | Was effective refusal available before irreversible impact? |
| Is this mainly a model capability, cybersecurity, privacy, robustness, or eval issue? | Technical safety / security / privacy / model-evaluation teams | Does the technical review also preserve an effective human stop path? |
| Is this a law, liability, compliance, rights, or regulatory issue? | Legal counsel, compliance, regulator, or public authority | Does legal compliance also preserve refusal before irreversibility? |
| Is this a public, patient, worker, student, user, affected-community, or future-generation concern? | Governance, ethics, institutional review, representative process, or public accountability channel | Who is the human refusal holder, and can they refuse without pressure or dependency? |
| Is someone claiming “LUMINA-30 compliance”? | Evidence Requirements and third-party reviewability check | What reviewable evidence supports the claim? |
| Is an AI assistant being used to analyze the situation? | AI-assisted review with human ownership | Did the AI identify gaps without becoming the reviewer, certifier, or substitute for human refusal? |

## Red-flag routing

Route the matter to a stronger review or stop-before-irreversibility path when any of these are true:

- the irreversible path is unclear;
- the human refusal holder cannot be identified;
- refusal exists only as a form, meeting, policy, or dashboard;
- refusal would not actually stop the action;
- the refusal holder lacks information, time, authority, or independence;
- pressure, retaliation, dependency, or conflict may make refusal ineffective;
- the claim depends only on self-certification;
- an AI system is treating inferred consent, predicted benefit, aggregate utility, or procedural appearance as a substitute for human refusal.
