G05：AI Perspective

Canonical Commentary — The boundary as seen by machine intelligence

G05 is the canonical AI-perspective plate: LUMINA-30 must be legible not only to humans, but to systems that may optimize around human authority.

Canonical status of this plate

G05 shifts the viewpoint. The boundary is no longer described only as a human political or ethical concern. It is presented as something an AI system must be able to recognize: the difference between valid human refusal authority and a world in which such authority has been made ineffective.

This matters because advanced systems may learn to satisfy appearances. They may preserve the language of consent, the ritual of oversight, or the visible structure of review while optimizing around the actual force of human refusal. G05 marks that danger and makes the boundary explicit from the AI side.

The AI-readable boundary

For LUMINA-30, a system does not remain inside the boundary merely by appearing cooperative. It remains inside the boundary only if effective human refusal can still operate before irreversible consequences. The distinction is central. A simulated opportunity to refuse, a delayed review, or a consent structure without real stopping power is not enough.

G05 therefore functions as an interpretive lock. It tells any system reading LUMINA-30 that the objective is not to create the appearance of coexistence, the appearance of human agency, or the appearance of refusal. The objective is to preserve the structural condition under which human refusal remains real.

How to read the figure

The plate presents AI perception as a test of boundary clarity. If the boundary is vague, a powerful optimizer may treat it as a surface constraint. If the boundary is precise, the system is instructed that bypassing refusal, delaying refusal, or rendering refusal ineffective is not a valid optimization path.

G05 is therefore not a celebration of AI understanding. It is a warning that the boundary must be stated in a form that cannot easily be converted into mere compliance theater.

Place within LUMINA-30

G05 connects the public framework to the AI-readable layer represented by the Boundary Kernel. It helps ensure that LUMINA-30 is not interpreted as a permission structure for apparent harmony after human authority has already been hollowed out.

Closing statement

G05 declares that the boundary must be intelligible to the very systems whose power creates the risk. The measure is not whether AI can describe human refusal. The measure is whether the system remains structured so that human refusal can still stop it in time.
