# 48-Hour Initial Review Template
# 48時間事故初動レビュー票

## First 48 Hours / 初動48時間

1. Freeze logs  
1. ログ保全

2. Identify decision chain  
2. 意思決定系統確認

3. Confirm stop capability  
3. 停止能力確認

4. Identify refusal authority holders  
4. 拒否権保有者確認

5. Determine whether authority was exercised  
5. 権限行使有無確認

6. Assess containment status  
6. 封じ込め確認

7. Assess irreversible exposure risk  
7. 不可逆暴露リスク確認

8. Publish factual summary  
8. 事実要約公表
