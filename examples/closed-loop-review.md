# Closed-Loop Review Failure

## Overview

An AI system was allowed to evaluate and approve its own outputs without independent human validation.

## LUMINA-30 Evaluation

### Critical Question

Was refusal authority structurally preserved?

### Findings

* Human review existed nominally but was bypassed
* System approval became self-referential
* No independent interruption path

### Conclusion

Closed-loop validation invalidates procedural authority.
