# Practical Glossary
# 実務用語集

- Refusal Authority  
  Human ability to reject, stop, or block operation in a way that remains operationally effective.  
  実運用上有効な形で、拒否・停止・遮断できる人間の能力。

- Procedural Validity  
  Whether governance procedure genuinely functioned before critical escalation.  
  重大拡張前にガバナンス手続が真に機能していたか。

- Evidence Integrity  
  Whether records are sufficient, preserved, and auditable.  
  記録が十分で、保全され、監査可能か。

- Responsibility Continuity  
  Whether responsibility remains traceable across organizations and stages.  
  組織横断・段階横断で責任を追跡できるか。

- Pre-Irreversibility Boundary  
  The condition before autonomous impact becomes too difficult to reverse.  
  自律的影響が巻き戻し困難になる前の条件。
