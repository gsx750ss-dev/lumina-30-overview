Flash Crash Incident (2010)

LUMINA-30 public HTML reference page.

Overview

A rapid market collapse occurred due to automated trading systems interacting in a feedback loop.

LUMINA-30 Evaluation

Critical Question

Was human refusal effective before irreversible impact?

Findings

  • Intervention existed but was not executed in time
  • System behavior exceeded human response speed
  • No enforced stop authority at the critical point

Conclusion

The system was procedurally invalid before the incident.
