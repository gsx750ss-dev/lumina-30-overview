## What this figure shows

G01 presents LUMINA-30 as a boundary framework rather than an ordinary policy, guideline, or ethical appeal.

---

## Relation to LUMINA-30

It introduces the central structural question: whether effective human refusal can still be exercised before irreversible consequences occur.

---

## Core message

LUMINA-30 begins at the boundary where human refusal must remain real, timely, and effective.
