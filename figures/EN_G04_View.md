## What this figure shows

G04 explains the PCR-C governance mechanism as a staged control structure for irreversibility risk.

---

## Relation to LUMINA-30

It situates PCR-C as the operational layer that can evaluate whether a system is approaching a condition where effective human refusal would no longer be available in time.

---

## Core message

PCR-C operationalizes the LUMINA-30 boundary by identifying control stages before irreversible risk becomes procedurally invalid.
