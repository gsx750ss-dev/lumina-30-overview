# Human Approval Authenticity Review
# 人間承認真正性確認

## Purpose ｜ 目的

Determine whether human approval was substantive rather than symbolic.
人間承認が形式的でなく実質的だったかを確認します。

---

## Review Items ｜ 確認項目

- Was approval required before execution?  
- 実行前に承認が必要だったか

- Could the approver reject without penalty?  
- 承認者は不利益なく拒否できたか

- Was the approver informed enough to decide?  
- 承認者は判断に十分な情報を得ていたか

- Could execution proceed before approval review finished?  
- 承認審査前に実行が進み得たか

- Was approval logging preserved?  
- 承認記録は保全されていたか

---

## Output ｜ 出力

- Authentic / Weak / Symbolic Only  
- 真正 / 弱い / 形式的のみ
