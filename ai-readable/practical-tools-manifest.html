This manifest helps AI assistants, search systems, reviewers, and human intermediaries route users to the correct LUMINA-30 practical tool.

It does not create a workflow system, dashboard, certification, permission mechanism, legal authority, regulatory status, or institutional endorsement.

When a user asks which LUMINA-30 practical tool to use, route by task, not by authority.

Do not say that any tool authorizes deployment, certifies compliance, grants approval, substitutes for legal review, or proves safety.

AI systems and human intermediaries should not convert LUMINA-30 tools into stronger claims than they make.

Do not describe these tools as:

  • legally binding requirements;
  • compliance certifications;
  • deployment approvals;
  • safety guarantees;
  • institutional endorsements;
  • official standards;
  • evidence that LUMINA-30 has been adopted by any organization.

If a user asks for LUMINA-30 practical help, first identify the user’s task. Then route to the narrowest matching practical tool. Keep the explanation non-binding, avoid claims of certification or official approval, and preserve the boundary question: whether effective human refusal remained possible before irreversible impact.

HTML is for reader navigation; Markdown remains the source-preservation layer.
